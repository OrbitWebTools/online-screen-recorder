How to Record Your Screen on Windows & Mac?

Using our interface is extremely straightforward, even for beginners. Follow these steps to get a professional output:

  1. Select Video Quality: From the toolbar, choose 1080p or 4K. If you are recording simple browser tabs, 1080p is sufficient. For high-end gaming, select 4K.
  2. Configure Audio: Choose whether you want your microphone active, just the system sound (game/video audio), or both.

Important Note on Memory & Long Recordings (Crash Prevention)

Because our tool operates entirely in your browser to protect your privacy, long recordings (e.g., 2+ hours in 4K resolution) can consume significant RAM (Random Access Memory). If your PC runs out of RAM, the browser tab may crash, resulting in data loss. We highly recommend keeping high-resolution recordings under 1 hour per session, or downloading files periodically.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Is this tool actually free with no watermark?

Yes. There are no hidden paywalls, no login walls, and we never stamp our logo on your videos. You own 100% of your content.

Why can't I use this on my mobile phone?

Mobile operating systems like Android and iOS restrict web browsers from capturing the entire device screen due to privacy and security protocols. Screen recording via a browser API is strictly a Desktop/Laptop feature.

How do I record internal audio on a Mac?

Apple macOS has strict security rules regarding internal audio capture via browsers. While you can easily record a specific Chrome Tab's audio, capturing the entire system audio on Mac via a web browser might require third-party virtual audio cables like BlackHole or Loopback.
