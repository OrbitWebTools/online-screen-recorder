How to Record Your Screen with Audio on Windows 10, Windows 11, Mac & Chromebook

  1. Open this page in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge on your Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC.
  2. Pick 1080p (or 4K for high-res displays), choose 60 FPS for gaming or 30 FPS for meetings, and select System Audio + Microphone.

Troubleshooting: System Audio, Black Screen & Video Formats

  • No System Audio in Recorded Video? When Chrome or Edge opens the screen picker dialog, look at the bottom-left toggle labeled "Also share system audio" (or "Also share tab audio") and make sure it is switched ON.
  • Black Screen on Netflix, Prime Video, or Protected Tabs? Streaming platforms use hardware DRM (Widevine) that instructs browsers to output black frames during screen capture. Normal websites, games, slides, and desktop apps record cleanly.
  • WebM vs. MP4 Playback: Modern Chrome and Edge record native MP4 when supported or high-efficiency WebM (VP9 + Opus). Both upload directly to YouTube, Google Drive, CapCut, and VLC Media Player without conversion.
  • Low-RAM PC Tip: If you are recording a 30+ minute meeting on an 8GB RAM laptop, choose 1080p · 30 FPS (or 720p · 30 FPS) so browser memory stays lightweight and smooth.
Privacy First: Because recording happens 100% inside your browser's local memory, remember to click Download Video before closing or refreshing the tab.
